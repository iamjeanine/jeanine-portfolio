## Short bio

Jeanine Emilia Cornillot is an Emmy- and Ambie-winning executive producer and showrunner whose work spans podcasts, television, scripted audio, practical AI, and emerging technology. She created and showran Scamfluencers and The Last City, co-developed and produced Dying for Sex, and founded Wondery's first AI Creator Lab. She is also the author of the memoir Family Sentence. At Ghost Mode Labs, she develops original IP and new tools for storytelling.

## Connecting thread

Jeanine Cornillot works across three connected practices: creating and showrunning narrative series, writing from lived and cultural experience, and testing how practical AI can support creative production. Across each, the throughline is the same: turning complex material into clear, character-driven stories and useful new formats.

## Full bio

Jeanine Emilia Cornillot is an Emmy- and Ambie-winning executive producer and showrunner with more than 300 episodes across podcasts, television, and digital media. She created and showran Scamfluencers and The Last City and co-developed and produced Dying for Sex, all for Wondery and Amazon.

Her television work includes serving as supervising producer on A&E's Born This Way, which won three Primetime Emmys, and Vox Media and PBS's No Passport Required, a James Beard Media Award winner. Her credits span true crime, narrative nonfiction, scripted fiction, pop culture, and children's storytelling.

At Wondery, Cornillot founded the company's first AI Creator Lab. She built its curriculum, internal resource hub, and partnerships, growing participation from four people to more than fifty across the company. Three projects were greenlit through the lab.

Through Ghost Mode Labs, she develops original IP and prototypes new ways to research, build, and extend stories across scripted, nonfiction, and emerging formats. Current work includes a visual audiobook that redraws itself with every listen, a 3D worldbuilding tool for writers, and a mythology research platform designed for franchise development.
