Work

Jeanine Emilia Cornillot

Cornillot’s work moves between true stories and invented worlds, from unscripted television and narrative podcasts to original audio fiction. At Ghost Mode Labs, she develops original IP and builds prototypes using AI and other emerging technologies to test new creative workflows for research, development, and production.

Productions Ghost Mode Labs

Productions

Television and podcasts, including unscripted series, narrative nonfiction, true crime, and original audio fiction.

Experience this chapter

  1. Scamfluencers

    Podcast · Wondery and Amazon · 2022 to present

    Creator and showrunner

    A weekly true-crime series about the internet’s most audacious scammers and the people caught in their stories.

    The series has 53 million downloads, reached number one on Apple Podcasts, and won the 2023 Ambie for Best Entertainment Podcast, with a second nomination in 2025.

  2. Dying for Sex

    Podcast · Wondery and Amazon · 2019 to 2020

    Co-developer and producer of the original podcast

    A narrative series about Molly Kochan leaving her marriage after a terminal cancer diagnosis and telling the story to her best friend, Nikki Boyer.

    The original podcast won the 2021 Ambie for Podcast of the Year. Its FX television adaptation won a Peabody Award, received nine Emmy nominations, and received a 2026 Television Academy Honor.

  3. The Last City

    Scripted podcast · Wondery and Amazon · 2023 to 2025

    Creator and showrunner

    An immersive 12-part science-fiction thriller starring Rhea Seehorn, set inside a utopian society surviving the climate crisis and designed natively in Dolby Atmos.

    Wondery’s first original series designed natively in Dolby Atmos, with sound shaping the story from the pitch and writers’ room onward. It reached number one in Apple Fiction in 20 countries and was nominated for the 2025 Ambie for Best Fiction Podcast.

  4. Born This Way

    Television · A&E and Bunim/Murray · 2015 to 2016

    Supervising producer

    A documentary series following seven young adults with Down syndrome as they build careers, independence, and relationships.

    The series won three Primetime Emmys and received 16 nominations.

  5. No Passport Required

    Television · Vox Media, PBS, and Apple TV · 2018 to 2019

    Supervising producer

    Chef Marcus Samuelsson travels the United States to explore immigrant communities redefining American food.

    The series won a James Beard Media Award.

  6. Hollywood & Crime

    Podcast · Wondery and Amazon · 2019 to 2024

    Senior producer

    Two seasons inside Wondery’s Hollywood true-crime anthology, including Billionaire Boys Club and The Execution of Bonny Lee Bakley.

  7. Life of Kylie

    Television · E! and Bunim/Murray · 2017 to 2018

    Senior supervising producer

    A documentary series following Kylie Jenner at the height of the Kardashian-Jenner media empire.

Ghost Mode Labs
