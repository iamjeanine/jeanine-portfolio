Work

Jeanine Emilia Cornillot

Jeanine Cornillot works across three connected practices: creating and showrunning narrative series, writing from lived and cultural experience, and testing how practical AI can support creative production. Across each, the throughline is the same: turning complex material into clear, character-driven stories and useful new formats.

Productions

Selected podcast and television work across nonfiction, scripted fiction, culture, and documentary storytelling.

Experience this chapter

  1. Scamfluencers

    Podcast · Wondery and Amazon · 2022 to present

    Creator and showrunner

    A weekly true-crime series about the internet’s biggest scammers and the people who fell for them.

    53 million downloads. Number one on Apple Podcasts. Winner of the 2023 Ambie for Best Entertainment Podcast, with a second nomination in 2025.

  2. Dying for Sex

    Podcast · Wondery and Amazon · 2019 to 2020

    Co-developer and producer of the original podcast

    A narrative series about Molly Kochan leaving her marriage after a terminal cancer diagnosis and telling the story to her best friend, Nikki Boyer.

    Winner of the 2021 Ambie for Podcast of the Year. The FX television adaptation won a Peabody Award, received nine Emmy nominations, and received a 2026 Television Academy Honor.

  3. The Last City

    Scripted podcast · Wondery and Amazon · 2023 to 2025

    Creator and showrunner

    An immersive 12-part science-fiction thriller starring Rhea Seehorn, set inside a utopian society surviving the climate crisis and conceived for Dolby Atmos from the start.

    Wondery’s first original series conceived and produced for Dolby Atmos from inception. Number one in Apple Fiction in 20 countries. Nominated for the 2025 Ambie for Best Fiction Podcast.

  4. Born This Way

    Television · A&E and Bunim/Murray · 2015 to 2016

    Supervising producer

    A documentary series following seven young adults with Down syndrome as they build careers, independence, and relationships.

    Three Primetime Emmy wins and 16 nominations.

  5. No Passport Required

    Television · Vox Media, PBS, and Apple TV · 2018 to 2019

    Supervising producer

    Chef Marcus Samuelsson travels the United States to explore immigrant communities redefining American food.

    Winner of a James Beard Media Award.

  6. Hollywood & Crime

    Podcast · Wondery and Amazon · 2019 to 2024

    Senior producer

    Two seasons inside Wondery’s Hollywood true-crime anthology, including Billionaire Boys Club and The Execution of Bonny Lee Bakley.

  7. Life of Kylie

    Television · E! and Bunim/Murray · 2017 to 2018

    Senior supervising producer

    A documentary series following Kylie Jenner at the height of the Kardashian-Jenner media empire.
