# Jeanine Emilia Cornillot: Work Index

> Jeanine Cornillot works across three connected practices: creating and showrunning narrative series, writing from lived and cultural experience, and testing how practical AI can support creative production. Across each, the throughline is the same: turning complex material into clear, character-driven stories and useful new formats.

## Productions

Selected podcast and television work across nonfiction, scripted fiction, culture, and documentary storytelling.

### Scamfluencers

Podcast | Wondery and Amazon | 2022 to present | Creator and showrunner

A weekly true-crime series about the internet's biggest scammers and the people who fell for them. 53 million downloads. Number one on Apple Podcasts. Winner of the 2023 Ambie for Best Entertainment Podcast, with a second nomination in 2025.

Permanent entry: https://ghostmode.studio/work.html#scamfluencers

### Dying for Sex

Podcast | Wondery and Amazon | 2019 to 2020 | Co-developer and producer of the original podcast

A narrative series about Molly Kochan leaving her marriage after a terminal cancer diagnosis and telling the story to her best friend, Nikki Boyer. Winner of the 2021 Ambie for Podcast of the Year. The FX television adaptation won a Peabody Award, received nine Emmy nominations, and received a 2026 Television Academy Honor.

Permanent entry: https://ghostmode.studio/work.html#dying-for-sex

### The Last City

Scripted podcast | Wondery and Amazon | 2023 to 2025 | Creator and showrunner

An immersive 12-part science-fiction thriller starring Rhea Seehorn, set inside a utopian society surviving the climate crisis. Number one in Apple Fiction in 20 countries. Nominated for the 2025 Ambie for Best Fiction Podcast.

Permanent entry: https://ghostmode.studio/work.html#the-last-city

### Born This Way

Television | A&E and Bunim/Murray | 2015 to 2016 | Supervising producer

A documentary series following seven young adults with Down syndrome as they build careers, independence, and relationships. Three Primetime Emmy wins and 16 nominations.

Permanent entry: https://ghostmode.studio/work.html#born-this-way

### No Passport Required

Television | Vox Media, PBS, and Apple TV | 2018 to 2019 | Supervising producer

Chef Marcus Samuelsson travels the United States to explore immigrant communities redefining American food. Winner of a James Beard Media Award.

Permanent entry: https://ghostmode.studio/work.html#no-passport-required

### Hollywood & Crime
