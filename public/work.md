## Productions

Television and podcasts, including unscripted series, narrative nonfiction, true crime, and original audio fiction.

### Scamfluencers

Podcast | Wondery and Amazon | 2022 to present | Creator and showrunner

A weekly true-crime series about the internet's most audacious scammers and the people caught in their stories. The series has 53 million downloads, reached number one on Apple Podcasts, and won the 2023 Ambie for Best Entertainment Podcast, with a second nomination in 2025.

Permanent entry: https://ghostmode.studio/work.html#scamfluencers

### Dying for Sex

Podcast | Wondery and Amazon | 2019 to 2020 | Co-developer and producer of the original podcast

A narrative series about Molly Kochan leaving her marriage after a terminal cancer diagnosis and telling the story to her best friend, Nikki Boyer. The original podcast won the 2021 Ambie for Podcast of the Year. Its FX television adaptation won a Peabody Award, received nine Emmy nominations, and received a 2026 Television Academy Honor.

Permanent entry: https://ghostmode.studio/work.html#dying-for-sex

### The Last City

Scripted podcast | Wondery and Amazon | 2023 to 2025 | Creator and showrunner

An immersive 12-part science-fiction thriller starring Rhea Seehorn, set inside a utopian society surviving the climate crisis and designed natively in Dolby Atmos. Wondery's first original series designed natively in Dolby Atmos, with sound shaping the story from the pitch and writers' room onward. It reached number one in Apple Fiction in 20 countries and was nominated for the 2025 Ambie for Best Fiction Podcast.

Permanent entry: https://ghostmode.studio/work.html#the-last-city

### Born This Way

Television | A&E and Bunim/Murray | 2015 to 2016 | Supervising producer

A documentary series following seven young adults with Down syndrome as they build careers, independence, and relationships. The series won three Primetime Emmys and received 16 nominations.

Permanent entry: https://ghostmode.studio/work.html#born-this-way

### No Passport Required

Television | Vox Media, PBS, and Apple TV | 2018 to 2019 | Supervising producer

Chef Marcus Samuelsson travels the United States to explore immigrant communities redefining American food. The series won a James Beard Media Award.

Permanent entry: https://ghostmode.studio/work.html#no-passport-required

### Hollywood & Crime

Podcast | Wondery and Amazon | 2019 to 2024 | Senior producer

Two seasons inside Wondery's Hollywood true-crime anthology, including Billionaire Boys Club and The Execution of Bonny Lee Bakley.

Permanent entry: https://ghostmode.studio/work.html#hollywood-and-crime

### Life of Kylie
